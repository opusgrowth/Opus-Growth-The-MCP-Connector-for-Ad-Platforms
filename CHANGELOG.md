# Changelog

Product milestones for Opus Growth, the MCP connector for ad platforms.

## 2026-08
- **Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)** cleared App Review and is live on every plan at no extra
  cost. Campaigns, ad sets and ads, carousel and video creatives, website and lookalike
  audiences, interest search, reach estimates, pixels and conversion events, Instagram post ads
  and insights. Creating a new lead form still needs a Meta permission we have not requested;
  attaching an existing form to an ad works.
- **Google Business Profile** is live. Manage locations, business information, hours,
  categories, photos and updates from chat.
- **LinkedIn Ads** and **TikTok Ads** cleared platform review and are live.
- **Enterprise plan** added ($299/month) alongside Pro and Agency.
- Free trial changed to **75 credits that never expire**, replacing the time limited trial.
- Billing moved to Paddle as merchant of record.

## 2026-07
- **TikTok Ads** integration completed, entered platform review.
- Panel: first-touch attribution & channel grouping for smarter growth analytics.
- Email preferences: one-reply unsubscribe honored end-to-end (KVKK/opt-out).

## 2026-06
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads** and **LinkedIn Ads** integrations completed
  (27 & 18 tools), in final platform review, opening soon.
- Multi-account management for agencies (Business Center / MCC).
- In-panel Ads Library.

## 2026-05
- **Google Ads** fully live, 37 tools: reports, audits, search-term waste analysis,
  campaign creation (Search, Performance Max, Demand Gen, App), bidding & conversions.
- Preview + confirm safety model and per-change budget guardrails.
- Hosted MCP connector: one URL, no terminal, no developer token.
